Kökzhol fact sheet

Temperature-controlled logistics for pharma, laboratory diagnostics and medical devices · Kazakhstan

Kökzhol (Көкжол) is a temperature-controlled logistics network for pharmaceuticals, laboratory diagnostics and medical devices in Kazakhstan. It runs two hubs in Astana and Almaty, serves 21 cities on four fixed route rings, carries three temperature classes with a data logger in every box, and is set up to EU and EAEU good distribution practice (GDP). The backbone starts on 15 December 2026.
