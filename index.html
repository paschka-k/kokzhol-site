The problem

Express is the exception. Today it is the plan.

In medical supply chains across Kazakhstan, a large share of the transport budget goes on express shipments that carry only a small share of the weight. Each one is booked by phone, packed by hand and tracked by hope. Kökzhol makes the scheduled cold chain the normal case.

Today

Ad-hoc express

Reagents leave when someone finds a courier. Temperature proof is a thermometer photo, if any. Two hubs often ship to the same city on the same day, separately.

With Kökzhol

A fixed day, one truck, one record

Every city has a delivery day on its ring. Loads from both hubs are consolidated. One logger per box writes the record; the platform shows it before the box is opened.

What changes for you

Express only when it is really needed

Routine replenishment moves on the timetable at a scheduled rate. Express stays available for the genuinely urgent case, with a clear reason attached.

Network

Two hubs. Four rings. One weekly backbone.

Astana and Almaty are the hubs. A weekly backbone of about 1,200 km links them. Four route rings leave the hubs on fixed loops and come back, so every city sees the same truck on the same day. Aktau, the furthest point, is 2,670 km from its hub.

Climate

At −45 °C the danger is frost, not heat.

Kazakhstan has a dry continental climate with a 97-degree span. A reagent that freezes is a reagent that is lost, so in winter the ambient class (+15…+25 °C) travels heated, and +2…+8 °C goods are protected from frost first and from warmth second.

FAQ

Questions labs and pharmacies ask first.

How does Kökzhol prove the temperature of each box?

Every thermobox carries its own data logger from packing to hand-over. The curve is read at the hub gate and at delivery, compared with the allowed band for the class, and attached to the delivery note as a temperature report. Customers download it by shipment ID from the platform. This applies from launch.

Why are +15…+25 °C goods heated in winter?

At −45 °C the risk for ambient goods is freezing, not warming. Plastic consumables and many reagents are destroyed by a single frost cycle. In winter mode the ambient class travels in insulated bodies with conditioned packs, and every ambient label switches to «heated» so nobody mistakes the box for a chilled one.

Which cities does Kökzhol deliver to, and how often?

Twenty-one cities from the hubs in Astana and Almaty, on four route rings plus a weekly backbone between the two hubs. Each city has a fixed delivery day on its ring. The West ring, the longest at 6,482 km, reaches Aktau at 2,670 km from the hub. The timetable is published at launch on 15 December 2026.

Can a lab book a shipment online and see the price first?

Yes, from launch. The customer portal shows the ring, the next delivery day and an indicative price for the chosen temperature class and volume before the slot is confirmed. Bookings route to the Astana or Almaty hub automatically, and tracking and the temperature report follow the same shipment ID.

Is Kökzhol open to companies that compete with each other?

Yes. Kökzhol is a neutral carrier. Laboratories, pharmacies, clinics, manufacturers and distributors, including direct competitors, use the same rings and get the same temperature record. Customer data is kept separate per account, and no manufacturer or customer name appears in another customer's documents.
